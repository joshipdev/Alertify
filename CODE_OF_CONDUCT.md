# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Alertify to be welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and code; do not attack people.
- Accept feedback graciously and focus on what is best for the project.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information, credentials, or private communications without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this code to the repository maintainers privately through GitHub. Do not make a public issue about a sensitive incident. Maintainers will review reports, protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical, and may edit or remove content or restrict participation when necessary.
